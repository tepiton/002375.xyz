# [{{ title }}](/)

A reading edition of *The Great Gatsby*, installed from [Project Gutenberg eBook #64317](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/64317). The text of the nine chapters is presented as it stands in that source. See the [colophon](/colophon/) for edition and license details.

Fitzgerald set two pieces of front matter before Chapter I. The dedication:

> Once again
> to Zelda

And the epigraph:

> Then wear the gold hat, if that will move her;
> If you can bounce high, bounce for her too,
> Till she cry “Lover, gold-hatted, high-bouncing lover,
> I must have you!”
>
> — Thomas Parke d’Invilliers

## Typography

Set in [p22-stickley-pro-text](https://fonts.adobe.com/fonts/p22-stickley-pro) and [neue-kabel](https://fonts.adobe.com/fonts/neue-kabel) via Adobe Fonts. Body text at a fluid size scaling between 16px and 20px. Line measure constrained to 48 characters.
